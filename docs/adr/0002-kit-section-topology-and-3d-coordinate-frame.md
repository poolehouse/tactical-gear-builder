# Kit Section Multi-Face Topology and Top-Down Flat-Lay Coordinate Frame

Every `MountSlot` on a Platform or mounted Component is assigned to a typed `KitSection` (`front`, `rear`, `left_side`, `right_side`, `sub_load`, `belt`) accompanied by a top-down bench flat-lay 3D coordinate frame (`normal: [0, 0, 1]`, `origin_3d`, `up_3d`). In this flat-lay orientation, the inner platebags face the floor (`-Z`), all exterior MOLLE faces the ceiling (`+Z`), the rear platebag (`rear_panel` / `rear_pals_field`, `y: 4..36%`) sits above the front placard (`front_placard`, `y: 44..78%`) connected by shoulder straps (`y: 36..44%`), and the cummerbund wings flank the front platebag. Switching the active `KitSection` in the UI filters the visible slots and layers for that face without resetting or mutating the underlying `LoadoutDraft`, while `All Sections` displays the complete non-overlapping flat-lay rig at once.
